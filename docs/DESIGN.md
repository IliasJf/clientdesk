# ClientDesk design direction

Style: Achromatic ledger, seed 2085767698. No redraws. The open-plane sidebar and canvas share a blue-grey ledger ground (#f2f4f7); working panels stay white. Geist is loaded from a local font file. Chrome is neutral; one blue mark belongs to cash collected, not decorative navigation.

Signature: the first screen connects the freelancer's work with money waiting to be collected. Project progress comes from completed tasks, invoice balances come from invoice lines and recorded payments, and the collection strip is computed from those same invoices. No invented growth rates or customer claims.

Overview asks: What work and money need attention? Clients asks: Who am I working with? Projects asks: What needs to move forward? Invoices asks: What is still unpaid? The invoice editor and preview keep the document beside its actions. Business settings provide the seller snapshot for new drafts.

Grid at 1440px: 232px sidebar + 2×36px canvas gutters + 1136px canvas. Main split: (1136−20)×0.62 + 20 + (1136−20)×0.38. At tablet width the sidebar folds; phone rows stack and dialogs use the available width. Counts, totals, and progress derive from source rows, with currencies never mixed into one money total.

Sample business: Morgan Creative. Sample clients: Northstar Studio, Lumen Coffee, Forma Architecture, Sora Goods. These are fictional portfolio records and must be replaced for real use. Their email addresses use example.com. No client communication is sent.
